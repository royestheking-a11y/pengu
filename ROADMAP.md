# 🗺 Pengu AI Product Roadmap (2026)

This roadmap outlines the major milestones and planned features for Pengu AI. Our goal is to build the most comprehensive and aesthetically pleasing Academic OS for students and experts.

## 📍 Vision
To empower students with elite academic tools and connect them with top-tier experts in a seamless, high-performance ecosystem.

## 🏔 Long-Term Goals (Vision 2030)
- **AI-Powered Syllabus Sync**: Automated study plan generation from uploaded PDF syllabi.
- **Global Expert Marketplace**: Expanding to 50+ academic disciplines.
- **Pengu 3D Ecosystem**: Immersive 3D environments for collaborative learning.
